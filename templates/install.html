Install TaskFlow on your phone or computer

TaskFlow is a web app: it installs from the browser in a few seconds, opens in its own window and can show notifications.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open TaskFlow in Safari (iOS 16.4 or newer).
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen”, then “Add”.
  4. Open TaskFlow from the new icon and sign in.
  5. In your profile, press “Turn on in this browser” and allow notifications.

Apple allows web notifications only for apps added to the Home Screen - that is why step 3 is needed.

Android

  1. Open TaskFlow in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots) and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
  3. Open it from the new icon and turn on notifications in your profile.

Computer

  1. In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  2. TaskFlow opens in its own window and appears in your start menu or dock.

Without internet the app shows a short page and tries again when you are back online.
