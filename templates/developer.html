API guide

Send the key in the “Authorization” header, as in the example. Answers are JSON. At most 120 requests per minute per person.

curl -H "Authorization: Bearer tf_…" {{ api_base }}/tasks?status=in_progress
RequestWhat it does
GET /api/v1/meYou, your role and the workspace of the key
GET /api/v1/projectsProjects of the workspace
GET /api/v1/tasks?status=&priority=&assignee=&project=&q=&limit=Tasks (with labels and custom fields)
GET /api/v1/tasks/<id>One task
POST /api/v1/tasks {"title", "description", "status", "priority", "assignee_id", "project_id", "due_date", "estimate"}New task
PATCH /api/v1/tasks/<id> {only the fields to change}Change a task
DELETE /api/v1/tasks/<id>Put a task in the trash
POST /api/v1/tasks/<id>/comments {"body"}Write a comment
